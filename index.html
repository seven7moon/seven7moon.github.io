ABOUT ME

Marketing execution backed by reliable measurement.

I am a digital marketer and data analyst focused on performance advertising, measurement and conversion optimization.

My work often combines Google Ads campaign management with GA4 and Google Tag Manager implementation, allowing me to look beyond clicks and understand whether campaigns actually generate leads, calls, registrations or sales.

I have worked across ecommerce, healthcare, local service businesses and lead-generation accounts, including Search, Performance Max, remarketing audiences, call attribution, ecommerce purchase tracking and offline conversion workflows.

Alongside paid media, I continue developing my capabilities in SQL, BigQuery, Python, statistics, Power BI and automation so I can connect marketing execution with deeper business analysis.
